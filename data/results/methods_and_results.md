We compared experimental and predicted structural inputs for ten targets whose PDB entries were initially released after 1 June 2023 and whose maximum detected query-normalized similarity to the available pre-cutoff PDB reference was below 0.50. One prespecified full-sequence ESMFold2 prediction per candidate was evaluated against the same experimental construct; qualification required measured TM-score ≥0.80 and Cα lDDT ≥0.75. Twelve of thirteen candidates qualified. The final ten were frozen before binder generation, using natural origin, lower detected pre-cutoff similarity and chain identifier as the ordering, with a pairwise redundancy exclusion at TM-score ≥0.80 under either normalization. The panel comprises seven natural and three designed proteins; target-prediction TM-scores were 0.8817–0.9857 and Cα lDDT 0.8225–0.9213.

We completed 100 attempts for each target × modality × input condition (ten targets, mini-proteins and nanobodies, experimental and predicted inputs), giving 4,000 attempts. Target sequence, residue mapping and chemistry were matched between conditions, and generation seeds and protocol settings were paired. Each attempt had five Boltz-2 refolds; all six endpoints used the same refold selected by 0.8 × design_to_target_iptm + 0.2 × design_ptm. All 4,000 attempts had all six measurements, with no missing scores. An independent audit checked identities, seeds, raw confidence scores, selected coordinates, hydrogen bonds, salt bridges and target-side buried surface area. The contact metrics retain legacy plip_ names but use Hydride/Biotite; the chemistry and area measurements use designed residues and exclude the nanobody framework from that mask.

We averaged attempts within each target and condition, then computed predicted-minus-experimental paired target differences with equal target weight. Intervals are pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 10,000 paired target-bootstrap replicates (seed 20261031; ten target pairs), without adjustment across the twelve endpoint/modality comparisons. Individual designs were not treated as independent target replicates.

Predicted inputs increased mean binder pTM by 0.0482 (95% CI 0.0202 to 0.0735) for mini-proteins and 0.0215 (0.0121 to 0.0311) for nanobodies. Nanobody hydrogen-bond counts decreased by 0.171 (−0.335 to −0.017). The other nine endpoint/modality intervals included zero; their estimates and intervals are shown below. These results do not establish equivalence or non-inferiority, and the larger target-specific changes remain visible in the per-target figures.

This comparison is conditioned on accurate target predictions and covers only ten selected targets. The structural-novelty reference contains 740,797 pre-cutoff PDB protein chains from a 16 September 2026 snapshot, with 9,317 known omissions (5,853 chains with at most five Cα atoms and 3,464 missing or unclassified chains). Foldseek sensitivity 9.5 and up to 10,000 candidates were followed by native non-fast TM-align on detected hits; this is neither an exhaustive pairwise search nor an exact reconstruction of a training manifest. Low detected global PDB similarity does not imply absence of shared local motifs. Distillation was outside the exclusion criterion, and no independence from all training sources is claimed. The endpoints are computational confidence or geometry proxies; no binding assay, experimental binder success or wet-lab validation was measured.
